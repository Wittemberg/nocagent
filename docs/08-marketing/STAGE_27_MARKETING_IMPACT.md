# Stage 27 — Marketing Impact

## Positioning
Stage 27 adds a very practical promise for small MSPs/MEIs:

> **Monitor the customer's Internet links and change the preferred WAN safely from the same platform.**

It connects physical inventory from Stage 26 with live network operation.

## Pain points
- “The customer says the Internet is slow and I need to log into the firewall.”
- “I have to remember which link is primary.”
- “Failover exists, but I don't know if the secondary link is actually healthy.”
- “I lose time accessing MikroTik/pfSense for simple WAN changes.”
- “Automatic failover flaps and creates more problems.”

## Value
- one view of both WANs;
- health, loss and latency;
- human-readable provider names;
- AI-assisted diagnosis;
- governed primary-link switch;
- automatic failover with anti-flapping;
- before/after audit;
- less remote troubleshooting time.

## Main message
> **Your customer's Internet links under the same operational control as the rest of the infrastructure.**

Supporting line:
> **Detect degradation. Validate the backup link. Switch safely. Audit everything.**

## MSP/MEI value
A technician can receive an alert on mobile, understand which provider is degraded and authorize a safe change without manually navigating vendor interfaces.

## Differentiation
Do not market as SD-WAN replacement or full firewall manager.

Market as:
- AIOps for small infrastructure;
- governed WAN operations;
- MikroTik/pfSense visibility;
- AI-assisted failover;
- integrated customer infrastructure control.

## Claims
Before Stage 27 is homologated: “planned/in development”.
After homologation: communicate only tested capabilities and supported device/version matrix.
