# Stage 27 — Campaign Concepts

## Campaign 1 — The customer called: Internet is bad
Headline:
**Before opening the firewall, ask InfraOps AI.**

Body:
See loss, latency, link state and which WAN is primary in one place.

## Campaign 2 — Safe failover
Headline:
**Primary link degraded? Validate the backup and switch safely.**

Body:
InfraOps AI can turn a WAN change into a governed workflow with precheck, postcheck and audit.

## Campaign 3 — AI operation
Headline:
**“Make Claro the primary link.”**

Body:
Natural language becomes a controlled Action — not an unrestricted terminal command.

## Campaign 4 — Autonomous continuity
Headline:
**Failover without flapping.**

Body:
Debounce, cooldown, hysteresis and recovery windows help prevent unstable links from causing repeated switches.

## Campaign 5 — Small MSP
Headline:
**Less time logging into firewalls. More clients under control.**

Body:
MikroTik, pfSense, backups, servers, inventory and AI in the same operational context.

## Visual direction
Use realistic small-business topology:
Internet A + Internet B → MikroTik/pfSense → switch → server/APs.
Avoid enterprise carrier/datacenter imagery.
