# Stage 27 — Sales Pitch

## Elevator pitch
> InfraOps AI brings MikroTik and pfSense into the same control plane as servers and backups. The technician sees WAN health, asks the AI what is wrong and, when authorized, can change the preferred Internet link through a governed Action with validation and audit.

## Demo story
1. Vivo shows 28% packet loss.
2. Claro is healthy.
3. InfraOps AI recommends changing primary WAN.
4. Technician approves.
5. Platform snapshots current state.
6. Changes preference.
7. Tests Internet/DNS/critical endpoint.
8. Confirms success or rolls back.
9. Audit records the change.

## Customer benefit
- faster response;
- less downtime;
- safer remote operation;
- proof of what was changed;
- better use of redundant Internet links.

## MSP benefit
- less vendor-interface navigation;
- standardized procedure across clients;
- remote response from one platform;
- future automation with policies;
- scale without losing governance.

## Objection: “Is this an SD-WAN?”
No. Stage 27 focuses on monitoring and governed operation of existing MikroTik/pfSense WAN configurations. It does not replace a full SD-WAN platform.

## Objection: “Does AI get firewall admin access?”
The AI expresses intent. Policy Engine, Action Registry, driver, precheck/postcheck and audit control execution. No arbitrary AI terminal is required.

## Phrase
> **From alert to safe WAN change — without leaving InfraOps AI.**
